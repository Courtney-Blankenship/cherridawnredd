    Meet Cherri

    Cherri Dawn Redd sings gospel with a big voice and an even bigger heart.

    You may have seen her on TBN, where she has sung for viewers across the country. For years she has led a ministry built on worship, encouragement, and prayer, and she loves lifting people up through music.

    Cherri has a studio album and several singles to her name. Right now she is in Nashville recording her next project, set for release in 2027.
